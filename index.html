TUR / TAR & Decision Rule

Test uncertainty ratio, test accuracy ratio, and guard-banded accept/reject limits for statements of conformity.

Ratios

TUR is the tolerance span divided by twice the expanded uncertainty of the measurement process — not of the reference standard alone. The standard's uncertainty is only one contributor; repeatability, resolution of the item under test and environment all belong in the denominator. Using the standard's accuracy alone gives TAR, which is the older and weaker measure.

Simple acceptance is only appropriate where the risk is agreed with the customer, and ANSI/NCSL Z540.3 requires TUR ≥ 4:1 for it. Below that, guard band or document the false-accept risk. ISO/IEC 17025:2017 §7.1.3 requires the decision rule to be documented and agreed before you issue a statement of conformity.
